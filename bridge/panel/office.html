Ask about this sheet.
The open sheet is sent with your question, so “what does column C do?”, “write me a formula that sums B by month” and “is anything odd in this data?” all work.

It is told it is answering inside a spreadsheet, so “what does CLEAN do?” means the Excel function — not the Python one.

It can also fill the sheet in: “create a 2026 monthly budget with a total row”, “put the months down column A”, “bold the header”. You get a preview of every cell it would touch and it changes nothing until you press Apply — with an Undo after, and the file only written when you press ⌘S.

Each question is asked on its own: the sheet travels with it, the earlier answers do not.
