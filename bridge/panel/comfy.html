Nothing is installed yet — here is what this does

This page turns a sentence into a picture or a short video clip, on this Mac, offline. It drives the ComfyUI engine that is already installed here, so you never have to touch a node graph — the ComfyUI tab stays available for that.

What it needs first are model weights, which are big files. Nothing is downloaded until you press a button, every size is stated before you press it, and the free space on the volume they land on is in the header above. Models you download here are shared with the ComfyUI tab — one copy, both surfaces.
