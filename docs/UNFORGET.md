### Detail - Session spillover

- **S9 — CLOSED 2026-08-29 (goose CLI slice 2).** Debi's words: *"every refresh wipes off the current session, plus it's not possible to surface any old/previous sessions. That shouldn't be the case."* Both halves are gone.
  **Files:** `bridge/pty_aider.py` (the generic detach/reattach machinery — relay thread, escape-safe ring buffer, grace timer, `attach_verdict`), `bridge/pty_goose.py` (the session store: `list_sessions`/`previews`/`store_size`/`remove_session`/`scan_session_id`/`reap_orphan`, plus `fence_env` extracted so no exec of the binary can forget the confinement), `bridge/routers/goose.py`, `bridge/routers/aider.py`, `bridge/panel/goose.html` (the strip), `bridge/panel/aider.html`.
  **The reload rule, stated once:** the PTY's lifetime is the PROCESS's, not the socket's.
